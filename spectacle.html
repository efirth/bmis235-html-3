Spectacle

We catch some good fish here. There's all kinds of kinds of fish in the lake: rainbow trout, tiger trout, largemouth bass, bluegill, yellow perch, brown trout, and pumpkinseed sunfish. We mostly catch rainbow and tiger. You can also have the option of where you want to fish: motor boat, the dock, the land, paddleboat, or kayak. The cabins we stay at are small and old, but become full of laughter and fun conversations.

If you visit, my favorite things to do are fishing out on the dock and eating dinner on the picnic benches ovrelooking the lake.
